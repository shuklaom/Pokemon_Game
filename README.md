In this assignment we were supposed to implement battles between trainers and the wild pokemons. Each pokemon has a moves priority value that is used to determine it's place in the attack lineup when battling. When battling, the accuricy of the pokemon is also based on random chance and if the pokemon manages to hit it's attack, that attack will be sent through a formula that will calculate how much damage that pokemon did to the other pokemon in the battle. This formula uses multiple values from the attacking pokemon's stats to calculate the attack damage. These stats are the Level of the pokemon, the power of the pokemon, the attack of the pokemon, and the defence of the pokemon. The battle of the pokemons have multiple types of moves each pokemon can use: Fight, Bag, Run and switch to a different pokemon.
